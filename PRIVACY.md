# Privacy policy

Tempesta (tempesta-browser-extension) collects no data.

- Bookmarks are read from and written to the `tempesta` bookmark store on the
  local machine, through the `tempesta` command line program registered as a
  native messaging host. The extension keeps no copy of them.
- The URL of the current page is read only when a shortcut, the toolbar icon or
  its menu is used, and only to save it as a bookmark.
- Nothing is sent to the author or to any third party. The only network
  activity related to the bookmark store is the optional `git` synchronization
  configured in `tempesta` itself, towards the remote chosen in its
  configuration.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code.
